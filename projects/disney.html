Consumer Products · Toy Design · Concept · Illustration

Incredibles 2 Mini Supers, collectible figures

Over a decade of professional work spanning consumer products, toy design, concept development, and illustration — primarily within The Walt Disney Company and alongside major industry partners.

This work ranges from award-winning toy lines like Doorables to concept design for experiential products, 2D surface design for licensed goods, and character illustration for global campaigns. The through-line is always the same: design that earns its place in a world already saturated with product.
