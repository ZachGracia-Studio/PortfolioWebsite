Disney

Toys, collectibles, licensed products and illustration

Toy Story Mini vehicles, concept sketches

For more than ten years I've designed toys, collectibles and licensed products, mostly at The Walt Disney Company and with partners like Mattel, Samsung, CASETiFY, Apple and Amazon.

This page collects some of that work, from award-winning collectibles to concept playsets, surface design and character art. Where I can, I've shown the process alongside the finished piece.

Art direction · with Moose Toys and Just Play · 2019

Doorables

I art directed Doorables, setting the stylization and the look of the entire line. With Moose Toys and Just Play, we built a mini-collectible style Disney could own. It won Collectible of the Year at the 2019 Toy of the Year Awards and is still going strong.

Pixar's Coco · with Mattel

Coco

A line built on Día de los Muertos, from figures to Mattel's Miguel guitar. As a Mexican-American designer, I treated cultural authenticity as a design requirement and carried it into every Pixar review.

Art direction and design · with Magic Jump · 2020

Frozen Playground

An open-air Frozen 2 inflatable built as Arendelle castle. I art directed and designed it with Magic Jump, working in red-line overlays: stretch the towers, let the windows shrink as they climb, and keep the shapes chunky at the base so they hold up as vinyl.

Concept design · Disney

Mickey expandable camper

Mickey’s camper opens into a whole campsite. The roof lifts into a tent, the front pulls out into an observation deck, and a giant Mickey glove holds the boat, then folds down into a picnic table. Concept sketches, with the play features called out.
