VNCE

A character built to carry its own world

Into the world
VNCE, in the beam
VNCE, world banner, establishing shot

VNCE is an original character I created in 2023. I'm building it to carry its own mythology, visual language and story across mediums.

The design is bold and graphic, made to move from paper to sculpture to screen without losing anything.

Right now I'm developing it across illustration, animation-ready model sheets and merchandise.
