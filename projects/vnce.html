VNCE — Full Character Illustration
VNCE — World Banner / Establishing Shot

VNCE is an original character created by Zach Gracia — not as a portfolio demonstration, but as IP with intention. Built to carry its own mythology, visual language, and narrative across mediums.

Where most characters exist to serve a project, VNCE exists to build a world. The character's design draws from the same design DNA as TAD — bold, graphic, made to translate from paper to sculpture to screen without losing anything.

VNCE is being developed across illustration, animation-ready model sheets, and merchandise. This is the beginning of something longer.

The Character

VNCE is designed with strict graphic discipline — every element of the design has to work in silhouette, in full color, and in black and white. Built to be versatile without being generic.
