About

ZACH
GRACIA

Creative Director · Product Design Manager

A dynamic Latino artist and designer working at the intersection of toy design, illustration, and original IP. Currently Product Design Manager at The Walt Disney Company's Consumer Products division, based in Orlando, Florida.

Zach Gracia is a dynamic Latino artist and designer hailing from California, currently working out of Orlando, Florida with his furry companions Charlotte and Beau — not to forget his partner-in-crime, Kelsey. With over a decade of experience in the toy and entertainment industry, Zach's journey has been filled with world-class creativity and category innovation, primarily in the realms of toy design, illustration, and animation.

What sets Zach apart is his knack for blending 2D and 3D design, creating captivating products that have generated over $500 million in global sales. His creative aptitude has led to collaborations with tech giants like Samsung, industry leaders like Mattel, trendsetters like Casetify, and household names like Apple and Amazon, just to name a few.

Currently, Zach works as a Product Design Manager for the Consumer Products division of The Walt Disney Company. Zach thrives on collaboration, bringing a strong teamwork mentality to everything he does, from building brands to art directing global product strategies.

Outside of his professional work, Zach is building TAD — an original designer toy brand rooted in the philosophy that the most compelling work lives at the edge of what can and can't be explained — and developing VNCE, an original character IP built to become its own world.
