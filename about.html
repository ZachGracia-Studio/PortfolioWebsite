About

ZACH
GRACIA

Artist · Designer · World Builder

I make toys, illustrations and characters, and lately whole worlds for them to live in. By day I lead creative work in Disney consumer products.

I'm a Latino artist and designer from California, working out of Orlando with my partner-in-crime, Kelsey, our son, and our furry companions Charlotte and Beau. I've spent more than 15 years in entertainment: toy design, illustration and animation.

I move between 2D and 3D, and the products I've worked on have sold over $500 million worldwide, with partners like Mattel, Samsung, CASETiFY, Apple and Amazon.

At Disney, my work runs from building brands to art directing products that ship around the world.

Outside Disney, there's TAD, my apparel and art print label, and VNCE, a character with a world growing around him.
