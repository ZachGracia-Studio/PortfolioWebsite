About

Blending 2D and 3D design to create worlds at the intersection of art, culture, and play.

Zach Gracia is a dynamic Latino artist and designer with over a decade of experience in the toy and entertainment industry. Today a creative leader in The Walt Disney Company's consumer products business, Zach has collaborated with Samsung, Mattel, Casetify, Apple, and Amazon — creating products that have generated over $500 million in global sales.
