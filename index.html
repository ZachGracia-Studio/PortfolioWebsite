About

I work in 2D and 3D, from illustration to sculpture to the finished product on the shelf.

I'm a Latino artist and designer from California, now in Orlando. I've spent more than a decade in the toy and entertainment industry, and today I'm a Product Design Manager in Disney's Consumer Products division. Products I've worked on have generated over $500 million in global sales.
