TAD is an original designer toy brand built on the belief that the most compelling objects exist at the edge of language — things you can feel before you can explain them.

Every drop is designed end-to-end by Zach Gracia: concept, sculpture, colorway, packaging. Limited runs. No restocks. Made to be held, not just displayed.
